# Intraday strategy research: comprehensive V3 discovery

**Decision:** The best current lead is a diversified opening-hour long portfolio selected by the joint state of 30-minute distance from the session low and 30-minute EMA distance, held for 240 minutes and closed the same day. It is a discovery-period, one-minute-bar execution proxy, not an established live profit or quote-executable strategy. The shorter-hold candidates are retained below because low-cost fills and capital recycling can make them useful.

## Scope and method

- Source: `D:\AlgoResearch\Quant-Pipeline-V3\runs\v3_comprehensive_20260923`. `EVIDENCE_COMPLETE.json` records 469,311/469,311 mandatory tasks complete. Discovery observations run **2025-05-01 to 2026-04-30**, 251 sessions. The user's approved discovery cutoff is **2026-05-31**, but this completed run contains no May 2026 research observations. No post-May data, replication, or final holdout was backtested.
- Screened the full saved 5-minute intraday pair surfaces: 15,051 feature-pair definitions at each of r3/r5/r10 for each of ten raw intraday targets, plus selected-cell specialist and temporal summaries. Examined every cell, including cells different from the summary-selected winner. Singles and short horizons were screened too. Filtered for sample count, symbol breadth, fold sign, nearby cells/resolutions, time of day, calendar, and independent same-symbol opportunities. A high overlapping observation count was never interpreted as independent trades.
- For the principal candidates, replayed one-minute raw opens after the signal decision **with one extra minute of entry delay**, then the governed intraday exit. All trades end within the regular session. The replay uses bars, has no NBBO, queue, partial-fill, market-impact, borrow, or limit-order fill model. Cost `c` is **total round-trip bps**, evaluated at `−1, 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5`: net trade bps = replayed gross trade bps − `c`. Negative cost is a hypothetical net rebate. It is not evidence that rebates or selective fills are obtainable.
- For the capital comparison, process signals chronologically; take a trade only if one of `K` equal-sized position slots is free; recycle the slot at exit; count idle cash as zero return. Ties are broken with five fixed hash seeds. Daily result is `(sum of selected gross trade bps − c × trade count) / K`. This is a capacity illustration with equal notional and no leverage or impact, not the production portfolio engine. Signals from correlated rules cannot simply have their returns added.

## Multi-leg and decision limits

An executed one-minute **beta-adjusted SPY short leg** on the primary signals produced 9,575 trades and **10.15 bps gross per stock-leg notional**, versus 13.50 bps for direct long. This removes some market exposure but costs a second executable leg and changes gross/capital exposure. It is an optional risk-control experiment, not a higher-return replacement. On the ten jump-short event dates, that short's daily results are negatively correlated with the main long rule in this discovery sample, but ten days are too few to allocate a permanent hedge.

The main 240-minute rule and the opening 30-minute rule have a **0.38 correlation** of equal-trade daily net-5-bps results on their 205 common dates. They also overlap on **612 symbol-days** (about 27% of the opening 30-minute rule's symbol-days). A combined portfolio must resolve same-symbol conflicts and shared slots before its profits can be estimated; summing their stand-alone modeled returns would overstate deployable capital efficiency.

The headline figures are selected from an enormous discovery search, so the displayed Sharpe, fold signs, and per-trade sample sizes are optimistic evidence. Correlated cross-sectional trades and common dates shrink effective sample size. [Multiple testing in return research](https://www.nber.org/papers/w20592) is a material concern here. There is no proof of live statistical significance or profitability after actual spread, fill probability, and market impact. The [SEC notes that limit orders do not guarantee execution](https://www.sec.gov/answers/limit.htm), so a modeled cheap or rebated fill must be tested jointly with its fill rate. The **next decisive validation** is frozen-rule quote/limit-order replay with realistic fill opportunity and capacity, then the user's separately reserved holdout. Keep May 2026 inside the user's authorized discovery boundary if that data is materialized later; never test June 2026 onward before the holdout decision.
