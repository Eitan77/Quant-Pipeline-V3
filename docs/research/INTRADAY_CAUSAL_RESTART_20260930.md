## Opening auction hypotheses

Temporary opening imbalances may reverse; durable stock-specific repricing may continue. Test a gap fade, a fade with an initial turn, and continuation with initial confirmation. The signal uses the first completed minute's gap relative to SPY, scaled by preceding 20-session close-return volatility. Entry follows the first five completed minutes at 09:35 ET; orders activate five seconds later. Both directions are allowed. Rank known standardized gap strength, use ten slots, and compare all eligible stocks with a fixed top decile of preceding five-session dollar turnover. No fitted ticker whitelist or realized-return ranking is used.

Historical corporate-action coverage misses several large price resets. Use actual regular-session closes/volume, require the immediately preceding market session, and exclude a factor-two current gap or a preceding 20-session lookback containing such a reset. This is a causal input-integrity rule, not a future corporate-action lookup. One hundred independently recomputed candidate signals and prior-history inputs agreed to numerical precision.

The liquid continuation hypothesis has 1,230 annual nominations, averaging 4.9 per market session. Before quote replay, 30/60/120-minute mean returns are 10.90/13.47/14.34bp at zero offset. Those bar projections substantially overstate subsequent quote results.
